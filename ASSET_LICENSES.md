### 3. Procedural Assets & Original Models

All 3D models and procedural geometry generators are original creations authored specifically for this project:
- **Player Human Rig & Capsule Collision**: Original geometry, textured with regional and casual attire variants.
- **Fictional 4x4 Off-Road Utility & Sedan**: Original fictional automotive design and physics chassis.
- **Bahraini Dhow-Inspired Coastal Workboat**: Original hull geometry and multi-point buoyancy setup based on public maritime heritage documentation.
- **Civilian Light Utility Helicopter**: Original aerodynamic airframe and rotor assembly.
- **Civilian Light Single-Engine Propeller Aeroplane**: Original general aviation design.
