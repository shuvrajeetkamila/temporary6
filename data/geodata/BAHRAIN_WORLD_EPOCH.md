# BAHRAIN WORLD EPOCH DEFINITION
## Model Epoch: 2026-10 (Current Modern Reality)

This document establishes the authoritative temporal and administrative epoch for all geodata, coastlines, road networks, urban massing, and marine boundaries in *BAHRAIN: THE ISLAND KINGDOM*.

---

### 1. Administrative Structure & Governorates

- **Target Administrative Epoch**: 2026.
- **Current Governorates (Four Sovereign Governorates)**:
  1. **Capital Governorate (محافظة العاصمة)** — Seat: Manama. Includes Manama, Juffair, Adliya, Seef, Diplomatic Area, Bahrain Bay, Nabih Saleh, and northern Tubli Bay.
  2. **Muharraq Governorate (محافظة المحرق)** — Seat: Muharraq. Includes historic Muharraq, Busaiteen, Arad, Hidd, Galali, Amwaj Islands, Diyar Al Muharraq, Dilmunia Island, and Bahrain International Airport.
  3. **Northern Governorate (المحافظة الشمالية)** — Seat: Sar / A'ali border. Includes Budaiya, Diraz, Barbar, Saar, Jasra, Janabiya, Bani Jamra, Hamala, Al Malikiyah, Jidhafs, Qal'at al-Bahrain (Bahrain Fort), Barbar Temple landscape, King Fahd Causeway Bahrain approach, Umm an Nasan, and Jidda Island.
  4. **Southern Governorate (المحافظة الجنوبية)** — Seat: Awali / Riffa. Includes East Riffa, West Riffa, Isa Town, Hamad Town, A'ali (Dilmun burial mounds), Sakhir (Bahrain International Circuit), Zallaq, Al Areen Wildlife Park, Awali, Jabal ad Dukhan (134m), Tree of Life, Askar, Jaww, Ad Dur, Durrat Al Bahrain, and the Hawar Islands archipelago.
- **Abolished Administrative Entities**:
  - The **Central Governorate (المحافظة الوسطى)** was officially abolished by Royal Decree in September 2014 and its territory was partitioned among Capital, Northern, and Southern Governorates. It is NOT present as an active administrative region in this build.

---

### 2. Coastline & Reclaimed Land Epoch

Bahrain's land area has expanded from ~660 km² historically to ~780+ km² through engineered land reclamation. The model reflects the 2026 coastline state:
- **Amwaj Islands**: Fully formed residential and marina archipelago northeast of Muharraq.
- **Diyar Al Muharraq**: Northern reclaimed land mass with primary road corridors and waterfront seawalls.
- **Dilmunia Island**: Health/residential island off Galali/Muharraq.
- **Bahrain Bay**: Reclaimed circular bay district north of Manama hosting modern commercial towers.
- **Reef Island (Lulu Island)**: Reclaimed residential island north of Seef/Manama.
- **Durrat Al Bahrain**: Complete crescent and atoll island group at the southern tip of the main island.
- **King Fahd Causeway Embankments & Border Island**: Bahrain-side western causeway embankments and Passport Island (eastern half).

---

### 3. Transportation & Transit Reality

- **Road Network**: Right-hand traffic (RHT) strictly enforced across all 4 governorates and causeways.
- **Public Transit**: Public bus network operating across major corridors.
- **No Operational Metro**:
  - Bahrain Metro is a planned transit project under procurement and engineering study; as of this epoch, construction has not yielded an operational rail service.
  - In strict compliance with product rules, **NO operational railway, metro, or tramway is simulated**. Public transit is serviced via original unbranded public buses.

---

### 4. Public Facilities & Sensitive Infrastructure Generalization

- **Bahrain International Airport (BAH / OBBI)**: Public exterior terminal massing, runways (12L/30R and 12R/30L), taxiways, and public roadway access accurately positioned. Sensitive operational interiors and military perimeters generalized as public massing with strict no-entry bounds.
- **Ports**: Khalifa Bin Salman Port (Hidd) and Mina Salman (Manama) represented as public exterior industrial and crane massing.
- **Petroleum & Refining Infrastructure**: BAPCO refining complex in Sitra/Awali and southern oil-field pump sites represented as generalized public exterior pipe/tank massing without operational data.
- **King Fahd Causeway Border**: Bahrain customs and border approach modeled as public exterior road infrastructure with a designated turnaround before foreign territory.
