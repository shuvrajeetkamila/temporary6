Vehicles

Summon puts a vehicle beside you. Boat and jet go to the nearest water or open ground if none is beside you. Keys 1–4 summon, V opens this list.

Get in with E when the prompt shows. Jet: hold Shift to throttle up, W nose down, A/D bank. Heli: Shift climbs, Ctrl descends, W flies forward.
